What is PropertyLime?+

PropertyLime is a property management app for keeping residents, payments, expenses, inventory, maintenance tickets and staff salaries organized in one workspace.

Who is it designed for?+

It’s designed for property owners and managers who want a clearer view of the people, finances and day-to-day work involved in looking after a property.

What can I manage in the app?+

Manage resident records for owners, renters and buyers; track payment status and expenses; organize inventory; follow maintenance tickets; and maintain salary records.

How can I get the app?+

Visit our get the app page for availability information, or contact info@intellibud.org for current access options.
