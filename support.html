How do I get started?+

Contact us for app access. Once you have the app, create an account, add your property details and begin adding residents and records.

What information can I organize?+

Resident details and payment status, property expenses, inventory items, maintenance tickets and staff salaries.

Can I track owners, renters and buyers?+

Yes. Resident records support owner, renter and buyer categories so you can organize the people connected to your property.

How do I get help with my account?+

Email info@intellibud.org with the issue you’re experiencing. For privacy or deletion requests, see our privacy policy.
